## [0.1.0]

- First release: `is_hex`, `parse`, `normalize`, `to_hex` and `HexColorError`.
- Support for `#rgb`, `#rgba`, `#rrggbb` and `#rrggbbaa`, with optional leading hash,
  any case, shorthand expansion and alpha round-tripping.
